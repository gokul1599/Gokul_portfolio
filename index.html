I believe theory finds its true purpose when tested in production. I build products to understand their underlying mechanics, iterate on user ergonomics, and continuously improve.

I'm Gokul, a computer science student focused on AI and software. I enjoy taking ideas, understanding the problem behind them, and turning them into products people can actually use.
