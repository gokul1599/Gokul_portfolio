TELUGUVA

“English in. Telugu out. Understanding made simple.”

An intelligent bilingual translation and accessibility platform that converts English documents, circulars, and notices into natural conversational Telugu with instant voice narration.

ROLE Creator · Accessibility & Frontend Engineer

01

THE PROBLEM & CULTURAL PURPOSE

Official government circulars, doctor prescription directions, school WhatsApp circulars, and electricity bills arrive predominantly in dense English. For native Telugu speakers and elderly family members, this creates real daily comprehension barriers.

Standard translation tools fail in two ways: they produce archaic, heavily Sanskritized Telugu that sounds unnatural in daily conversation, and they fail to provide slow, patient audio pronunciation for elderly citizens with low literacy or deteriorating eyesight.

I built Teluguva to eliminate this barrier: a camera-enabled scanner that extracts text on the user's phone, translates it into warm, conversational Telugu, and reads it aloud with adjustable speeds.

03

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

LAYER 01

Client OCR Engine

Tesseract.js Web Worker extracting English document text directly inside the browser sandbox without transmitting private bills to external cloud vision APIs.

LAYER 02

Context Transformation

Rule-based and LLM-assisted linguistic adapter substituting rigid textbook grammar with common Telugu loan words understood across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

LAYER 03

Speech Synthesis (TTS)

Native Web Speech API integration with customized prosody, pause insertion, and rate switching tailored for senior citizen auditory comfort.

LAYER 04

Accessible Touch UI

Built with Next.js 16 and Tailwind CSS v4 featuring 44px+ minimum touch buttons, high-contrast text ratios, and instant font scale toggle (A, A+, A++).

04

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Why In-Browser OCR?

Utility bills and clinic notices contain sensitive personal details. Running Tesseract.js locally in the browser processes document OCR on-device, eliminating the need to send private image files to external servers.

Why 'Simple Telugu' Mode?

Formal Telugu translations are often as impenetrable to ordinary speakers as English. Replacing 'సమర్పించవలసిన రుసుము' with 'కట్టాల్సిన ఫీజు' makes the software immediately useful in real households.

Elderly UI Design Constraints

Eliminated complex nested menus in favor of a clear dual-pane comparative view. Users can tap any individual English word to see its phonetic Telugu breakdown.

Next.js 16 with Turbopack

Next.js 16 App Router enabled instant static shell delivery with zero client runtime overhead for non-interactive structural components.

05

CHALLENGES & LESSONS LEARNED

CHALLENGE

Elderly users often photographed crumpled bills under dim household lighting with camera motion blur, causing standard OCR models to produce gibberish.

SOLUTION

Engineered an HTML5 Canvas image pre-processing filter that auto-boosts local contrast, applies threshold binarization, and warns the user if lighting is insufficient before processing.

LESSON

Accessibility is not just software styling — it is understanding the physical environment and human constraints of the person holding the phone.
