01

THE PROBLEM & GEOSPATIAL PURPOSE

Planning sacred pilgrimages and heritage explorations across India is a chronically frustrating ordeal. Information is fragmented across outdated regional tourism portals, conflicting Darshan hours, unverified dress codes, and commercial travel blogs with broken coordinates.

Travelers who wish to plan journeys that respectfully combine sacred temples, ancient rock-cut caves, UNESCO monuments, and natural waterfalls face an absence of source-grounded tools. Furthermore, general-purpose AI chat assistants routinely hallucinate opening hours, invent imaginary darshan ticket links, and suggest physically impossible mountain itineraries.

I engineered DevYatra / Templeora to provide a grounded, truthful alternative: an atlas of India spanning all 36 states and union territories across 9 distinct geographic vectors (Temples, Heritage, Caves, Hills, Waterfalls, Lakes, Nature, Beaches, Wildlife), backed by a context-scoped route planner that uses verified facts.

02

9 GEOGRAPHIC DISCOVERY VECTORS

DevYatra organizes destinations into 9 strictly typed and source-attributed categories, allowing users to discover both major monuments and lesser-known geographical treasures:

🏛️ Temples & Shrines

Ancient Shaivite Jyotirlingas, Vaishnavite Divya Desams, Shakti Peethas, and medieval stone sanctuaries with verified Darshan windows.

🏰 Heritage & Forts

UNESCO World Heritage monuments, medieval Rajput and Kakatiya granite citadels, Mughal palaces, and architectural ruins.

🗿 Caves & Monoliths

Ancient basalt Buddhist chaityas, monolithic excavations like Kailasa at Ellora, and natural karstic speleothems at Borra.

⛰️ Hills & Passes

Western Ghats shola peaks, tea valley highlands, Nilgiri biosphere ridges, and Himalayan trans-mountain passes up to 5,300m.

🌊 Waterfalls

Spectacular multi-tier cascades, segmented monsoon torrents like Jog Falls, and deepest rainforest plunge basins like Nohkalikai.

⛵ Lakes & Wetlands

High-altitude saline lakes at 4,350m (Pangong Tso), Ramsar wetland lagoons (Chilika), and floating phumdis (Loktak Lake).

🌿 Nature & Canyons

Indigenous living root bridges, deep granite river canyons (Gandikota), alpine flower sanctuaries, and seasonal salt deserts.

🏖️ Beaches & Coast

Blue Flag certified coastlines, sacred pilgrim sea ghats, laterite geo-heritage cliffs, and tranquil island atolls.

🐅 Wildlife Reserves

Project Tiger reserves, the world's primary habitat for the One-horned Rhino, and the last sanctuary of wild Asiatic Lions at Gir.

03

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

LAYER 01

Explorer UI & MapLibre

Next.js 16 and React 19 client with MapLibre GL vector maps, custom dark cartography tiles, and responsive mobile bottom sheets.

LAYER 02

Geospatial Database

Serverless PostgreSQL on Neon managed via Prisma ORM with spatial index coordinates, state/district taxonomy, and relational joins.

LAYER 03

Provenance Engine

Multi-tier data classification tagging records as GOVERNMENT_SOURCE, VERIFIED_OFFICIAL, TRUSTED_SOURCE, or UNVERIFIED with last-checked timestamps.

LAYER 04

Context-Scoped AI Planner

Groq API LLM planner restricted to database-verified destinations, computing realistic day-by-day travel legs and Darshan timings.

04

ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Why Neon Serverless PostgreSQL + Prisma?

Geospatial relationships (State → District → Sub-district → Shrines) demand strict relational integrity. Neon scales compute to zero during idle periods while providing instant query performance.

Why MapLibre GL over Google Maps SDK?

MapLibre GL is completely open-source, lightweight, eliminates expensive per-load commercial API billing, and supports fully customizable dark obsidian map themes that match the studio aesthetic.

Zero-Hallucination Route Planning

Itinerary planning requires accurate timings and grounded stops. The route planning engine selects stops strictly from existing database rows with verified ground truth.

Transparent Provenance Tags

Rather than claiming absolute completeness, DevYatra / Templeora labels source provenance transparently: government gazette records, verified temple board notices, and approximate coordinates are clearly marked.

05

CHALLENGES & LESSONS LEARNED

CHALLENGE

Displaying hundreds of geographic markers on mobile devices caused severe thread lock and sluggish scrolling during zoom gestures.

SOLUTION

Implemented supercluster vector clustering on MapLibre and decoupled the active place drawer state using lightweight client-side spatial bounding box filters.

LESSON

In geospatial software, performance depends heavily on data hierarchy: filter coarsely at the regional level before streaming detailed coordinates to the client.
